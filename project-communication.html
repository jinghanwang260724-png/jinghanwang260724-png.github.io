FIELD RESEARCH

表达与接收之间Between speaking and hearing

说的人常用的表达，与听的人希望收到的回应，是否一致？从评价、请求、拒绝、表达不满和反驳五类情境出发，把同一件事放到两种视角里。Do the responses people tend to give match the responses they prefer to receive? Five contexts—evaluation, requests, refusals, dissatisfaction and disagreement—are examined from two perspectives.

同一情境，两份问卷One scenario, two questionnaires

关系一般的同学展示挑选很久的衣服或球鞋。A 卷问表达者会如何评价，B 卷问接受者更能接受怎样的回应。首页中的“你自己觉得好看就好啊”来自第 6 题；它不是一种固定的心理标签。A classmate shows an item they took time to choose. Questionnaire A asks what the speaker would say; B asks what the listener would prefer. The homepage sentence comes from item 6. It does not carry a single, predetermined psychological meaning.

我的工作My contribution

作为项目负责人统筹 4 人跨专业团队，参与双视角问卷设计，使用 Excel 与 SPSS 整理数据、统计分析及分组对比。团队回收 430 余份问卷，获得 344 份有效问卷。As project lead, I coordinated a four-person interdisciplinary team, worked on the paired questionnaires, and used Excel and SPSS for data preparation, statistical analysis and group comparisons. The team collected over 430 responses, with 344 valid questionnaires.

如何阅读这个研究Reading the research

比较的是表达习惯与接受偏好，不是给某句话评出绝对的好坏。问卷附录已核对；现有材料不包含原始答卷与统计输出，因此页面不把首页互动做成群体比例或研究结果。The comparison concerns habits of expression and preferences in reception, rather than an absolute rating of a sentence. The questionnaire appendix has been checked. Raw responses and statistical output are not part of the supplied materials, so the interactive example does not claim to display study findings.
