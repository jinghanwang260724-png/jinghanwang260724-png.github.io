从行为描述，到人格标签From an action to a label

在 Christine 的盘问场景中，措辞被重新表述，提问权也影响了她可以怎样解释自己。论文关注具体行为如何被压缩成一个固定的人格判断。后来解释的场景，让先前的评价再次成为问题。In Christine’s cross-examination, wording is reformulated and control over questioning shapes the explanations available to her. The essay examines how a specific action becomes a fixed judgement of character. Her later explanation puts that earlier evaluation back into question.

同一个人，不同的评价标准One person, different standards

Forrest 在学校与军队中的评价形成对照：机构不同，判断能力与价值的标准也不同。这里对照的是两组场景与评价者，不是虚构一句在两个地方重复出现的台词。Forrest’s treatment at school and in the army shows how institutions apply different standards of ability and value. These are distinct scenes and evaluators, rather than the same line repeated in two settings.

我的方法与交付Method and work

对照影片与剧本，建立四组场景分析，联系语言意识形态框架，独立完成 5 页英文课程论文。比较措辞、身份标签、视觉线索与叙事安排如何共同塑造可信度。I compared the films and scripts, analysed four scene groups through a language-ideology framework, and completed an independent five-page English course essay. The comparison considers wording, identity labels, visual cues and narrative framing.

分析的边界Limits

研究对象是少量经过编写的电影场景，不是对真实观众的实验，不能推广为所有电影的普遍规律。论文保留了关于概念整理、翻译、语法与措辞方面的 AI 辅助说明。The material consists of a small number of scripted scenes, not an experiment on real audiences or a universal claim about cinema. The essay includes a disclosure of AI assistance with preliminary conceptual organisation, translation, grammar and wording.
