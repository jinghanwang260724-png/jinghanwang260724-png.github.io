BRAND / STRATEGY

一颗蓝莓，可以连接什么？What can a blueberry connect?

“莓”好吉乡：从长白山鲜果出发，把产地直销、生态采摘、自然研学与加工产品放进同一份农旅融合计划。A Changbai Mountain blueberry strategy connecting direct sales, picking experiences, nature learning and processed products.

01 / 鲜果01 / Fruit

梳理市场资料、竞品与消费意愿，从产地资源与不同客群的需求出发。Reviewing market information, competitors and consumer intentions, starting with local resources and the needs of different customer groups.

02 / 体验02 / Experiences

围绕亲子家庭、研学团体、生态休闲游客与特色农产品消费者，策划采摘节、亲子研学等体验。Planning picking festivals and family learning experiences around families, student groups, ecological leisure visitors and specialty-food consumers.

03 / 品牌03 / Brand

参与品牌传播卖点、渠道规划与节日礼盒等方案，串联产品、体验和可以带走的产地记忆。Working on brand messages, channel plans and seasonal gift proposals to connect products, experiences and memories of the place.

交付与状态Deliverable and status

团队交付为 64 页项目计划书。客流、营收和落地目标属于计划，页面展示策划结构与个人工作，不将其写作已实现业绩。The team delivered a 64-page project plan. Visitor, revenue and implementation targets are projections; this page presents the proposed strategy and my work, not achieved commercial results.
